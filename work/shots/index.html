SHOTS!

A bar at closing time. The bartender is the house, the house is neutral, and nobody knows what is poured. You say which glass you want, out loud and for keeps, and then you find out what everyone else did about it.

A game of nerve under no information, built in Godot and exported to the browser. No install and no account: it runs on a desktop browser or a phone held sideways.

Play Play it in the browser Nothing to install. Repository Source Godot project and GDScript.
